Brief Description

Devoted Domestics’ brand-new website establishes instant trust for a home cleaning business, with a booking-first UI/UX, real client testimonials, and a lead-capture flow built to turn first-time visitors into scheduled cleans.

Time

3 Weeks

Team

3 Members

Platforms

Web

Type

Local Service Website

Industry

Home & Domestic Services

Idea

A Home Services Brand Needed a Home Online

Devoted Domestics had already earned real, repeat clients on trust and reputation alone. The idea: a site that felt just as warm and dependable as the cleaners themselves, so a stranger's first impression matched the service already proven in person.

Challenge

Word-of-Mouth Only Goes So Far

Every enquiry arrived through Instagram DMs or a phone call, with no consistent way to explain services, share real reviews, or capture a lead outside business hours. Anyone comparing cleaning companies online found nothing to click on.

Solution

A Booking-First Website, Built to Convert

A single-page site structured around the exact questions a new client asks: what do you clean, is it safe, and how fast can I book? A trust-building hero, six service cards, a simple 3-step process, real testimonials, and a lead form feeding straight into the enquiry inbox.
