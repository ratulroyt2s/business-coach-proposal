01 — The Festival

Understanding Durga Puja

What is Durga Puja?
A five-day Hindu festival honoring the Goddess Durga's victory over the buffalo demon Mahishasura. In Kolkata, it becomes a citywide celebration of art, ritual, and community — recognized by UNESCO as an Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2021.
When is Durga Puja 2026?
October 1–5, running from Shashthi through Vijaya Dashami. Mahalaya arrivals begin September 27 for guests on the Immersion and Connoisseur packages.
Can non-Hindus or foreign visitors take part?
Yes. Durga Puja is an inclusive, citywide celebration — pandals are open to everyone, and our guests are warmly welcomed into rituals like Sindur Khela and the Anjali offering alongside local families.
What should I wear?
Modest, breathable clothing is appreciated at pandals and temples — covered shoulders and knees, especially for the Ashtami Anjali offering. Light layers work well for warm days and cooler evenings.
Is photography allowed?
Most pandals welcome photography, but it's courteous to ask before photographing individual worshippers up close, particularly mid-ritual.

03 — Travel From the U.S.

Getting Here

For flight-time tables by gateway city, e-Visa guidance, and more, see the full U.S. Travelers Guide.

Do U.S. citizens need a visa for India?
Most U.S. travelers apply for an India e-Visa (eTourist visa) online before departure. Requirements can change, so always confirm current rules with official Indian government sources or your travel advisor before booking flights.
How long is the flight from the U.S. to Kolkata?
Typically 19 to 24 hours from major U.S. gateway cities, with one or two connections through hubs like Doha, Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Frankfurt, or Delhi.
When should I book my flights?
Aim to book 3–4 months ahead for the best fares and seat availability into the festival window, and confirm your e-Visa is in hand before ticketing.
What's the time difference?
India Standard Time is roughly 9.5–10.5 hours ahead of the continental U.S., depending on your coast and daylight saving. We recommend arriving a day or two before Mahalaya to settle in.
04 — While You're There

On the Ground

Is Kolkata safe during Durga Puja?
Kolkata is widely regarded as one of India's calmer, more welcoming major cities, and Durga Puja itself is a citywide family celebration. Groups are capped at eight guests and privately guided throughout, so you're never navigating the festival alone.
Will there be a language barrier?
No — English is widely spoken in Kolkata, and your guide and cultural concierge are both fluent English speakers with you for the full journey.
What's the weather like in October?
Expect warm, humid days in the mid-80s to low-90s°F, with cooler, breezier evenings — especially at the pre-dawn Mahalaya ceremony. Light, breathable layers work best.
Can you accommodate dietary restrictions or health needs?
Let us know when you enquire — our concierge can accommodate most dietary needs and will share practical health and water-safety guidance ahead of your trip. We recommend consulting your doctor for personalized travel health advice before departure.
What should I budget for beyond the package price?
International flights, your India visa, personal spending, and travel insurance. Everything else in your chosen package — accommodation, guiding, and the meals listed — is covered.
