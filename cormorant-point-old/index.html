Welcome to Cormorant Point Hotel

Nestled along the waterfront, Cormorant Point Hotel has been welcoming guests since 1987 with gracious hospitality and breathtaking views. Whether you are visiting for a romantic getaway, a family vacation, or a corporate retreat, our dedicated staff is committed to making your stay memorable. Our hotel features 120 well-appointed guest rooms and suites, a full-service spa, two on-site restaurants, and direct beach access.
