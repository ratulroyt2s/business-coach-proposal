About Puja Kolkata

By Invitation.

We are a small, Kolkata-based team who believe Durga Puja should be entered, not observed — and we've built our entire practice around that one conviction.

Our Story

Why We Started

Puja Kolkata began with a simple frustration: watching visitors arrive for the world's greatest festival and leave having seen it only from the far side of a barricade — a spectacle glimpsed between someone else's shoulders, narrated by a guide who had never once stood inside the pandal committee's inner circle.

We grew up inside this festival. The relationships that make a different kind of journey possible — with the artisans of Kumartuli, with pandal committees who have run their puja for generations, with the families who invite a stranger to sit at their table on Ashtami — aren't something a tour company can build in a season. They're built over years. We've spent ours building them, so our guests don't have to.

What started as hosting friends-of-friends through the festival became, gradually and deliberately, a small private practice. We kept it small on purpose — because the moment a group grows past a handful of people, the doors that matter start to close.

"A festival this vast can still be entered quietly — if the people leading you in have actually been let inside."

What We Believe

The Principles Behind Every Journey

I
Access Over Spectacle
We measure a journey by how close it lets you stand, not by how much it lets you see from a distance.
II
Small By Design
Eight guests, never more. It's the only way intimacy and genuine access both survive contact with a city of five million.
III
Relationships, Not Vendors
Every artisan, committee, and family who welcomes our guests is someone we've known for years — not a contact we called last week.
IV
Priced Honestly
In U.S. dollars, in full, from the first quote you receive. No hidden additions once you've committed.
